## Introduction

<p>It's a high-gain device that amplifies the difference between two input voltages.  It is also used to perform arithmetical operations like summing, inverting, differentiation, integration, etc. on any given input signal. </p>

<p>If no voltage is supplied to it, ideally, the opamp would give infinite gain. In order to prevent this, we give a voltage supply at pin 4 and 7. If input goes beyond this voltage supply, output gets clipped.
</p>
